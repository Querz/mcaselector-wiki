The UI language of the MCA Selector can be dynamically changed in the settings.
The following languages are available:

* English
* German
* Czech (thanks to [@mkyral](https://github.com/mkyral) for the initial translation)
* Spanish (thanks to [@NathanielFreeman](https://github.com/NathanielFreeman) for the initial translation)
* French (thanks to [@SkytAsul](https://github.com/SkytAsul) and [@DoctaEnkoda](https://github.com/DoctaEnkoda) for the initial translation)
* Hungarian (thanks to [@Tyoda](https://github.com/Tyoda) for the initial translation)
* Italian (thanks to @valeilsimpaticone for the initial translation)
* Japanese (thanks to [@Uguisu64J](https://github.com/Uguisu64J) for the initial translation)
* Korean (thanks to [@Saebyul1221](https://github.com/Saebyul1221) for the initial translation)
* Dutch (thanks to [@Clijmart](https://github.com/Clijmart) for the initial translation)
* Polish (thanks to [@Marcinolak](https://github.com/Marcinolak) for the initial translation)
* Portuguese (thanks to [@D3W10](https://github.com/D3W10) for the initial translation)
* Brazilian Portuguese (thanks to [@cr1st0ph3r](https://github.com/cr1st0ph3r) for the initial translation)
* Russian (thanks to [@Quarktal](https://github.com/Quarktal) for the initial translation)
* Swedish (thanks to [@TechnicProblem](https://github.com/TechnicProblem) for the initial translation)
* Turkish (thanks to [@EReaXe](https://github.com/EReaXe) for the initial translation)
* Ukrainian (thanks to [@gigsoll](https://github.com/gigsoll) for the initial translation)
* Simplified Chinese (thanks to [@LovesAsuna](https://github.com/LovesAsuna) for the initial translation)
* Traditional Chinese (thanks to [@hugoalh](https://github.com/hugoalh) for the initial translation)

If you want to contribute a translation, you can find the language files in
[resources/lang/](https://github.com/Querz/mcaselector/tree/master/src/main/resources/lang). The files are automatically detected and shown as a language option in the
settings dropdown menu once they are placed in this folder.
